Who Is
Vincent Rubinaccio?

Vincent Rubinaccio is a two-time Emmy Award-winning creative producer, director, and storyteller with over 15 years of experience building campaigns that move audiences at scale.

His work spans professional sports, national brands, broadcast television, and social media — creating content for the Boston Bruins, Boston Red Sox, Fenway Sports Group, NESN, and Barstool Sports, among many others.

Vincent founded 17 Productions on a simple belief: every client deserves broadcast-quality storytelling, regardless of budget or category. From Emmy-winning playoff campaigns to luxury real estate films to cinematic wedding videos — the standard never changes.

He brings the same Emmy-winning precision, creative instincts, and problem-solving mindset to every project he touches. Fluent in English and Spanish. Open to in-person, hybrid, and remote engagements.
